## 2. Game Data

Idle AI stores game-related data locally on your device.

This may include:

- Game progress
- Levels
- In-game currencies
- Unlocked rooms
- Machines
- Bots
- Research progress
- Upgrades
- Settings

This data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The developer does not have access to your locally stored game progress.

If you uninstall the app or delete its application data, your locally stored game progress may be lost.

---

## 3. No User Accounts

Idle AI does not currently use its own user account system.

You do not need to provide personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or date of birth to play the game.

---

## 4. Advertising

Idle AI uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

The game may contain optional rewarded advertisements. You may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward.

Advertising services are provided by Google.

Although Idle AI itself does not collect or store personal data on developer-operated servers, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process certain technical information in order to provide advertisements.

This may include information such as:

- IP address
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers, where available
- App interactions
- Advertising interactions
- Diagnostic information
- Device and application information

This data is processed by Google and is not stored on servers operated by the developer.

For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Rewarded Ads

Idle AI may offer optional rewarded advertisements.

Watching rewarded advertisements is voluntary.

After successfully watching a rewarded advertisement, the player may receive an in-game reward.

Google AdMob is used to provide these advertisements.

## 6. Internet Connection
